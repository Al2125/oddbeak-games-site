GAME INFORMATION

About the game

A cozy fantasy visual novel where winning a debate can be more important than winning a battle.

Accidentally traveling to another dimension is no easy feat. Especially when your arrival destroys a castle and you can't afford to pay for the damage.

To pay off your debt, you'll have to become the kingdom's new mediator. The problem? You have absolutely no idea how to do that.

Luckily, the Debate for Dummies manual has all the rules you need to know. Use what you've learned to craft the perfect response, turn your opponents' arguments against them, and settle absurd disputes you never should have been dragged into in the first place.

Learn the rules. Win the debate.

Every debate puts what you've learned to the test. Analyze your opponent's arguments, find a rule you can use to your advantage, and choose the right words to craft your response.

Your performance matters.

The kingdom needs stability. The castle needs order. You need to get home.

Every debate has only two possible outcomes: win or lose. And the results could have consequences.

A kingdom with quirky characters.

Not everything is about conflict. Between absurd debates and unexpected situations, you'll meet a cast of strange but lovable characters. And perhaps you'll find something you weren't even looking for: a place to call home.

Key features

  • An original debate mechanic. Learn the rules and find the right argument.
  • Multiple endings based on your performance.
  • A cozy story filled with quirky characters.
